We are recruiting interns, MSc, PhD and post-doctoral students with a passion for field robotics and machine learning, to work on high-impact applied projects. We post job openings from time to time and accept unsolicited applications.

Successful candidates typically possess several of the following qualifications:

- Strong background in mobile robotics (**perception**, **localization**, **mapping**, **planning**, and **control**);
- Knowledge of computer vision and multimodal sensing (LiDAR, RGB-D cameras, IMUs, sonar, radar, multispectral);
- Basic understanding of deep learning and reinforcement learning for robotics;
- Interest in path planning, constrained optimization, and/or mapping and localization (SLAM);
- Proficiency in **C++** and Python, ideally with **ROS/ROS 2**;
- Experience with robotics simulators (Gazebo, Isaac Sim, Unity, **Unreal Engine**, **AirSim**, etc.) and visualization tools (**RViz**, **Foxglove**);
- Familiarity with embedded or distributed computing (real-time systems, communications, power management, memory);
- Ability to work in multidisciplinary teams and enthusiasm for field experimentation;
- **A plus:** experience deploying ground, aerial, or aquatic robots in real-world environments;
- **A plus:** ICRA, IROS, RA-L published papers.

Beyond technical skills, we value curiosity, initiative, scientific rigor, and a genuine enthusiasm for advancing the state of the art in autonomous robotics.
