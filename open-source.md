At the SAFiR Lab, we believe that reproducible research accelerates scientific progress. Whenever possible, we release the software, datasets, and tools developed in our research to support the robotics community and facilitate the adoption, validation, and extension of our work.

<p><a class="button is-safir" href="https://github.com/SAFiRLab" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Our full GitHub repositories</a></p>

<h2 class="title is-4 section-heading">Featured projects</h2>

- **[ARENA](https://github.com/SAFiRLab/ARENA)**: an adaptive risk-aware multi-objective framework for UAV 3D path planning.
- **[RRT-Rope](https://github.com/SAFiRLab/rrt-rope)**: a fast shortest path planning approach, integrated into the Open Motion Planning Library (OMPL).
