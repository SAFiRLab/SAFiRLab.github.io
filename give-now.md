Advances in robotics are driven not only by ideas, but also by the people and resources that bring them to life. Your support directly contributes to the development of the next generation of autonomous robots capable of operating in real-world, complex environments that have a significant impact on society.

Donations to the SAFiR Lab help, among other things, to:

- support master's and doctoral students;
- purchase specialized equipment and sensors;
- develop open-source software and datasets;
- fund scientific outreach and training activities;
- accelerate the completion of research projects in autonomous robotics.

Donations to the SAFiR Lab are made through the **University of Sherbrooke Foundation**.

### Tax receipts

An official tax receipt is issued for all donations of $10 or more, in accordance with the policies of the University of Sherbrooke Foundation.

### Thank you

Every contribution, no matter how large or small, helps advance research, train the engineers and researchers of tomorrow, and develop technologies that will have a tangible impact on our society. We sincerely thank you for your support of the SAFiR Lab.
